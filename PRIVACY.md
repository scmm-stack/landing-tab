# Privacy Policy

Landing Tab has no account, no analytics and no server. The developer receives no data from it.

## What stays on your device

Your shortcuts, groups, settings, search history (if you turn it on) and images are stored in your browser. They are not sent to the developer or to anyone else. Removing the extension removes them. A backup is a file you create and keep yourself.

## What leaves your device

Only these requests are made, and each one is started by you:

| Action | What is contacted |
| --- | --- |
| Opening a shortcut or submitting a search | That website or search engine, as with any link |
| Adding a shortcut, if website images are on | That website's home page, and the icon files it names, which may be on another domain the site uses, as on any visit. No cookies and no referrer. |
| Choosing **Extension library** as the wallpaper | GitHub, which hosts the extension's code: the list of library images at most once a day, and each image once, with no cookies and no referrer. Images are then kept in your browser. |

Website images need your permission once. **Settings > Shortcuts > Fetch website images automatically** turns them off and hands the permission back.

## Permissions

- **Current tab:** reads the address and title of the page when you click the toolbar button, to add it as a shortcut.
- **Search:** sends a query to your browser's default search engine when you choose that engine.
- **Storage and unlimited storage:** keep your shortcuts, settings and images in your browser.
- **Bookmarks (optional):** imports and exports bookmarks when you ask it to.
- **Website access (optional):** fetches a website's icon when you add a shortcut.
